## Full description (4000 chars max)

Tally is a calm, minimal daily calorie counter for people who eat mostly the
same things and just want the number.

No food database to search. No account. No ads. No cloud. Open the app, tap
your usual burrito, done — the big total ticks up with a satisfying animation
and you get on with your day.

**Log in two seconds**
Type calories and an optional note, or one-tap your regulars from the Menu —
your saved staples with calories (and optional protein) attached. Had the
same thing twice? Tap ⊕ on the entry and it counts ×2.

**A day that matches your life**
Days roll over at 2 AM, not midnight — the 1 AM snack counts toward tonight,
where it belongs. Forgot to log yesterday's dinner? Add it to yesterday.

**A target, not a judgement**
Set a daily calorie target and the total lives inside a progress ring.
Sparkles gather as your day fills; hitting the target earns a small, tasteful
burst. Go over and the ring turns a calm amber — nothing ever shouts at you.

**Streaks that reward showing up**
Log at least once a day and your streak grows — with a little celebration
each day you keep the chain. Streaks reward logging, never restriction.

**Protein, only if you want it**
Flip one switch in Settings to track protein grams and a daily protein
target. Leave it off and the app is pure calories.

**Your data is yours**
Everything stays on your device. Export a one-file backup anytime; import it
on a new phone and continue where you left off. No sign-up, no sync, no
servers, nothing to leak.

**Details that add up**
History with a 7-day trend chart and weekly recap. Light and dark themes.
Undo for deletes. Edit anything, including the time. Works offline, always.

Tally is for people who quit heavyweight diet apps but still want the
number. It weighs 3 MB and respects you.
