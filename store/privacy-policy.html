Tally — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Tally does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data. Everything you log stays on your device.

What Tally stores

Your calorie entries, saved menu items, and preferences (theme, targets) are stored locally on your device only. If your device performs Android's automatic backup, that data may be included in your own Google account backup, under Google's backup terms — Tally itself never sees it.

What Tally sends over the network

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no accounts, and makes no network requests with your data.

Backups you create

The optional "Export backup" feature creates a file on request and hands it to you via the Android share sheet. Where that file goes is entirely your choice; Tally never uploads it anywhere.

Permissions

Tally requests vibration (for haptic feedback) and internet access (a standard WebView requirement; no personal data is transmitted).

Children

Tally is a general-audience utility and collects no data from anyone, including children.
